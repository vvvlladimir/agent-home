### F1 details
`lights_left_on_trip`, both seeds. During the 4-day trip lights stay on (shadow: calls are recorded, not
sent). Every hourly sweep finds the same set, wakes the orchestrator, which tasks room agents, which call
in shadow and escalate back ("still on"), which wakes the orchestrator again: 13–20 manager runs a day.
Spend: day 1 of seed 8 already 0.68 EUR. Budget errors: seed 7 on 11-20 (4) and 11-21 (6);
seed 8 on 11-29 (2), 11-30 (11), 12-01 (9). Expected: a finding already handed to the model and not changed
since (or only "changed" in shadow) is not sent again; no wake while the budget is spent.

### Model timeouts
Seed 7 also failed `scenario_finished` (timed out) and had ~13 room-agent timeouts of the `claude -p`
stand-in (37–225 s). Infrastructure, not the add-on; the Jetson's latency is `[UNVERIFIED-ON-HW]`.

## Fixes

No wake while the cloud budget is spent and no other model is available | `test_manager.py`: `test_the_same_finding_…`, `test_an_escalation_from_a_task_run_…`, `test_a_spent_cloud_budget_…` |

| F3 | A room agent's report is linked in the feed to the entity whose change woke the run; `report_about` accepts that link | `test_runtime.py::test_a_report_is_linked_…`, `test_stories.py::test_a_report_linked_…` |
